Gluttony

The Maw of Hell

_________

For those who consume more than they need, depriving those who require those resources of them for their own pleasure, they initially believe they got it good. A luxury cruise on a liminal structure where they are provided everything they could ever want by the beautiful sea angels and handsome gorgons. It’s practically a reward for their excess in life at first, more heaven than hell. Those who demand more than they are given, or those who become suspicious and peek behind the curtain begin seeing cracks in the facade. The demons seem too excited to serve them, the food begins to suspiciously taste exactly the same, the restrictions become a bit too secretive. Those who never find out the truth find their stay lasts forever, eventually passing peacefully in their sleep. The demanding lot are taken to the lower levels to meet their demands and are never seen again.

To those who want answers? They find they’ve been kept as livestock. Fattened up for slaughter, plated and served to other sinners. They have an ultimatum; work and help punish those like themselves - becoming a protected lost soul - or die. But their death isn’t the same as the others. They may notice everyone has a similar severity in their actions. That’s because the apparition needs his fill too. He feasts on the worst of the worst and any who could blow the operation.

Sea Angels

Sea angels are everything a sinner could want in a sea monster: beautiful and sentient with a lovely voice and a demure personality. They service the sinners in any way they desire outside of closed doors. They are the waiters, the room-service, the maids, the chefs, and the hosts. Sinners can make requests of them, and they’ll obey. The problem comes when a sinner demands beyond what a sea angel has been instructed. When a sinner demands more than their fair share. When this happens, sea angels are trained to politely refuse and tell someone higher up. If attempted to be forced upon them despite this, they’re trained to kill the offending sinner on the spot as an example.

Truth be told, a better name for sea angels would be sea monsters. Beneath the deep cloaking magic of the Gluttony Ring turning a feast of flesh into a michelin star meal, sea angels - if they aren’t a lost soul who has retained their form - are mish-mashes of rotting sea creature parts that don’t match. Except for their mouths, filled with needle teeth. They are the butchers. They are the executioners. Be polite to them if you want your death to be peaceful.

Gorgons

Gorgons are humanoid amphibious demons, despite their reptilian appearance. While notable for the snakes on their head - unlike what legends about them claim - they are unable to turn victims to stone. Gorgons are inherently incapable of harnessing potent magic of that variety. Instead, gorgons have complete and total control over the extra heads sprouted from their scalps. They’ve earned the nickname “panopticon” from other demons due to their staggering amounts of eyes and what they’re used for. Observance.

Gorgons act as bouncers and muscle in the eternal cruise that is the Gluttony ring. While they handle the heavy labor, their eyes focus on the sinners. Watching their actions, their relationships, their needs, their curiosity. They are the security cameras in a place with none. They keep the sea angels and the Reaper updated on this information. They are ironically some of the safest demons in this ring - they’re rarely the ones in charge of butchering. They are just the sinners’ herding dogs.

Apparition of Gluttony, Lexi Sonata

The siren race in the freshwaters of Weodirrus recently fell into extinction with Lucifer - oft shortened to just Lexi - being the last of his kind. Due to being such a rare – and dangerous – beast, an aquarium eventually found and captured him. In this aquarium, Lexi found himself lonelier than ever before and starving. He was treated as a feral animal, despite being just as smart as humans. The staff assigned to his exhibit were instructed to wear sound canceling headphones due to his constant hypnotic singing, in a futile attempt to lure in prey. One day, Lexi forcibly broke through the glass of his tank, starving and hostile, and was finally shot dead by security. The final river siren of Weodirrus killed.

In the Gluttony ring, Lexi is finally able to satiate his appetite, despite his body remaining scarred from his years of starvation. The sinners in his ring are trapped in what seems to be a luxurious cruise surrounded on all sides by river rapids, waited on hand and foot by sea angels and gorgons alike, but once the facade is pulled back the true danger sets in. The sinners there are treated as cattle for slaughter, should they demand too much, should they look for more. The best fate is being butchered and cooked to serve other gluttonous pigs. The worst is meeting Lexi face to face. They won’t make it to the cutting board most of the time; he prefers his meals living.

Reaper of Gluttony, Jade Luthuli

Jade hates talking about their life while they were alive. All they'll say about it was that it was one trainwreck after another with everyone expecting them to cope with it. Their work consisted of odd jobs to just keep themself alive, while their life went to shit around them. Jade found it next to impossible to care about – or even for – themself. Life always threw all the shitty things their way, so what was the point? One fateful day, running on very little sleep (or so they claim), Jade allegedly accidentally walked into the train tracks before work, and that was it. No one was able to save them in time before the train came. Their disaster of a life ended in one fell swoop.

When they woke up in the luxury suite in the ring of Gluttony, they recognized things… no, they had changed. Once a normal human, now they were reptilian with their locs morphed into live snakes and their hands flayed. An angel - or perhaps another demon - sat them down and explained, “You are in hell, but not to be punished. Follow me, there is much we have to discuss.” The second chance they didn’t know they wanted had been granted, being gifted the power to torment those who made their life a living hell. Most sinners try to avoid their gaze, it’s rumored that if you meet their eyes then you are marked for death.

Wanderer of Gluttony, Neptune Calaveras

Neptune never liked being a face in the crowd, a woman meant to serve those who wanted more and more until their overconsumption killed them. Most of her waking life was spent like this, and her death was looking to be the same until the wanderer before her retired. This gave her a practically magical opportunity; rather than making sure the ring ran smoothly, she’d get to deliver some damned souls to the ring instead. Of course there was the tiny issue of her being the reaper at the time, but that was cleared up and off she went to the world she so adored.

Gluttony was a sin rarely called on, so Neptune went under the radar. The woman of many faces she was called, impossible to track but you’d know when her work was done. She’d put on the mask of a simple employee or servant to go even further under the radar and slaughter those who thought they could simply waste what others needed so dearly. Those who knew her during those moments said they never would’ve guessed, and the terror she strikes in the hearts of the unprepared who were used to the more passive wanderer has resulted in a mostly positive change in the world, as no living tool or poison can kill a wanderer.
