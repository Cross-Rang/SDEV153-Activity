Anger

Hell’s Hunting Grounds

_________

Anger is a normal emotion; one of the many ways for a soul to cope with the world’s unwanted stimuli. On its own, anger is not a sin. Actions taken while angered must be accounted for in this judgement however. Anger is not an excuse, it is an explanation. For those who let their wrath consume their every waking moment and take it out on those around them, their ultimate fate is one similar to what they inflicted on those who surrounded them. Walking on eggshells, navigating a forest where light never comes and passages always seem to loop. One may see shadows moving in ways they shouldn’t, giant beasts dragging the dead to their final resting place, and other sinners desperately fighting for an escape.

The apparition is one no sinner has been able to describe. Large, but silent. Some claim it too is a shadow, others that it's another roaming beast. Nothing about it is human, not its actions, behavior, stature, noises… nothing but its intelligence. Once it locks onto a sinner, it acts slow. Isolating them, stalking them, but going just enough under the radar that those marked for death rarely notice it is the one after them. Not until it’s too late. No witnesses have ever seen the apparition in action, all they know is that - whatever it is - it doesn’t eat the bodies. It just lets them rot.

Shadows
Type: Ethereal
Role: Hazard
Creation: Native

Shadows are exactly what they sound like. Shadows. They aren’t exactly alive, nor are they exactly sentient. They’re just… there. Any sinner unfortunate enough to wander into them will be hit with a nasty surprise as they’re just as alive as any other demon, and equally as blood thirsty. Many sinners have met their end smothered in darkness, freedom just steps away.

Despite a seemingly lack of individuality and a soul, many shadows do come to show a personality in due time. Some will even puppet the corpses of sinners to better display this, although it’s never a permanent ordeal.

Kaiju

Type: Undead
Role: Working
Creation: Native

When we hear Kaiju, we think of lizards the size of skyscrapers and powered by radiation. When anyone in hell hears of Kaiju, they think of lizards the size of a multi-story house powered by bloodlust. So not that far off from the living’s interpretation. While the most common kaiju is a giant reptile, other forms of kaiju exist, such as massive rodents and land fish (the land fish variety can only get up to the size of a single story house, they are rather small). These demons amble about the ring on four legs or two, searching out sinners to sate their permanent rage. It is rare to meet a kaiju who doesn’t have something they passionately loathe.

Death by kaiju is one of the worst fates for sinners as they seem easy to avoid at first. All you have to do is hide, right? They are big, and most sinners aren’t. Hiding seems plausible… until they recognize in dawning horror that they are essentially trying to hide from a massive bloodhound. Once a kaiju has picked up an unnatural presence, the sinner has three options; fight, flight, or freeze. Oddly enough not many attempt to fight kaijus.

Apparition of Anger, Valentine Arania
Type: Undead
Role: Apparition
Creation: Lost

Valentine’s mind is hazy about its past, but it knows just enough about it to remember roughly how it died. It was during the plague era of a kingdom it can’t recall, twenty years after its sister had been executed. It would often participate in fighting circles to deal with its rage at life, but through a severely weakened immune system, it caught said plague - a prion disease spread between humans. Valentine refused to quit fighting even as it became more dangerous, and infected most - if not all - of its opponents. Likely, it was only finally killed when the kingdom burnt down, and it was sent to hell.

Valentine was initially sent to the Ring of Anger as a victim - a wild animal to be hunted. It was scared, obviously, as it woke up with no idea of who it was or where it was - just that it was in danger. Maybe it was this fear and obliviousness that allowed it to slaughter the previous apparition, and once it had recovered enough to know what was happening it changed the rules. The fear it’d felt in the moment was exhilarating. The forest is permanently engulfed in night, looping to a point bearings are impossible to grasp. The cloak it wears is a permanent reminder to sinners of why it gained the position - they don’t have the strength to kill an apparition of anger, it did.

Reaper of Anger, Elshine Uranus
Type: Ethereal
Role: Reaper
Creation: Native

When you can’t distinguish shadows from reality, your mind races. It tries to comprehend what you’re staring at, who you’re staring at, and often you get it wrong. In the anger ring, distinguishing what’s real and what isn’t is the difference between life and death. Elshine is the result of someone guessing horribly, horribly wrong. Originally indistinguishable from any other shadow, it was mistaken for another sinner, one it’d just killed and partially eaten. It possessed his body to kill these sinners too, although the care and love they had for him stuck with it more than it anticipated. They kept his - now her - body with some… augments to stay convincing.

Eventually, she took the name Elshine Uranus to fit in with the other reapers - she hadn’t even realized she’d become a reaper, she was too focused on the attention she was getting from sinners - although she still doesn’t quite fit in with them. She didn’t have an identity before she put on that corpse, she was just a trick of the light made deadly. But they still show her the care she deserves. Every time she’s mistaken for a sinner by another, she becomes so giddy, but anything more than a mistaken identity flings her into a blind, homicidal rage. She knows they aren’t people she wants attention from; they despise people like her.

Wanderer of Anger, Orson
Type: Undead
Role: Wanderer
Creation: Native

Orson has been dead so long he’s forgotten what his life had even been. His own body is bloated and rotting, having been that way for years. Analyzing the living, he’s grown disdainful and hateful over what they see as beautiful. The perfect, plastic skin, the round innocent eyes of a child, a body deprived of what it needs to survive, it’s all so repulsive to him. But even worse is how they treat those outside of it. They think they’re so intelligent when they throw away those who don’t waste their lives in pursuit of a temporary goal. It’s no wonder that most victims of the kaiju have been found without their heads.

Even more disturbing is what he is capable of. He doesn’t simply hunt with chains and contracts; Orson is known as The Zombie Lord due to the potent diseases he can craft to cull the souls he hunts. These diseases rot the brain, rot the body, but force it to remain alive until it physically can’t move anymore. The outbreaks he’s caused have always claimed the lives of millions. Entire kingdoms fallen, villages burnt to ashes, all without him raising a finger. The armies he controls are obedient to his every word even should they not hear him. “Anything above the neck is useless.”

Inspiration
  • The ring itself is based of the ROM Hack 'the AKA Coronation Day. I am extremely facinasted by that game and wish to fully understand it eventually.
  • Each of the character designs are meant to invoke, "Absurdly rich hunters who decided to go after humans next" as seen in Ready or Not
  • Valentine was based on the concept of, "What if CWD could infect humans?" As you can see, nothing fun! It's dead now!
  • Elshine is a nod to the trope of an eldritch horror residing in a cute body.
  • Orson is heavily based on Heroes of Might and Magic V necromancer hero Orson. I kept picturing him as a frog or fish in my head but my friend compared him to a naked mole rat and... yeah.
