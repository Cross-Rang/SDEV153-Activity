Treachery

Hell’s Circus

_________

Treachery is a ring for betrayers and backstabbers, however the ring itself has a long history of not being run the best. It was only a century ago that a stable apparition was established only because the only way to kill him formed on the inside of a maggot sized-hole in his face. If the queen had her way, this apparition situation would continue, however it’s… easier to have an apparition who’s practically invulnerable in this ring. People treat the ring of treachery as a circus for entertainment, a view that the ring has leaned into by allowing people to watch their torture of traitors. They don’t just want to punish traitors; they want to completely break them.

Anything is permitted for the workers of the ring to do to the sinners, so long as it doesn’t stoop to murder, husk-creation, or sexual abuse. Unlike the other rings, nothing can prepare you for what the demons of this ring have in store for you. These acts are logged and reviewed, with the apparition selecting certain ones that an audience would find the most entertaining. Only at these shows are sinners allowed to die. Acts gone wrong, on-stage punishment, an overeager crowd, and the grand finale where enough blood is gathered to coat the stage. Getting turned into an immobile puppet may be a blessing in disguise, then.

Liches

The humans of hell, and just as horrible as they are in the living world. These demons take the form of various mannequins - or simply kick the soul out of the puppets and take over those bodies - and stretch skin over their frames to mock their living counterparts’ desperation for conformity. They seem to be the only demons who put more than an ounce of thought into their own living counterparts, finding all life disgusting and something to mock.

These vessels harness magic deemed forbidden by living magic communities. Spells of immortality, love, harm, reanimation, you name it and a lich has very likely figured out how to use it. They mostly use this magic on themselves to further their lifespan and sinners to harm them. Other demons are safe from this magical experimentation… mostly. Some bad actors are among the liches, but then again, can’t you say that about everyone?

Soul Weavers

Soul Weavers are giant bugs that control emotions, with all the horror that entails. While unable to control the soul itself, they can build and block parts of it to ensnare the owner’s emotions. The soul’s owner is always aware something is wrong, something has irreversibly altered, but is unable to feel the way they did prior to this shift. This is often saved as a manipulation tool to convince them to listen to the demons in charge and they can change them back, but this too is a lie. Once a soul has been changed, there is no undo button. Manually changing it back is just as risky.

Puppets

Puppets are lost souls of the worst sinners in the Treachery Ring. They’ve become trapped inside a cage of their own making, unable to move, unable to speak, only able to stare and be posed to the apparition’s liking until their end. This used to be a much slower, terrorizing process. The apparition had mercy on those who came after him, though. Now it only takes a few seconds for a full conversion.

Apparition of Treachery, Belial Iscariot

In life, Belial was the servant of an infamous queen, although no one truly knows what happened. Even while alive, he had an affinity for dark magic, and using his highly-statused position he’d beckon younger servants to his chambers where they’d be slaughtered so he could bathe in their blood and preserve his youth. Of course, all this death was pinned on his master, so he could continue his rituals. One day, he requested a favor from three separate wanderers: for the neighboring kingdom to burn to the ground over a slight committed against his master. This led to an early death - beheaded by revolutionaries for his ties to the corrupt royalty.

When Belial got to Hell, the reapers of treachery had a task for him. He had a limited amount of time before he became just another wooden doll for the apparition’s collection - as did all other sinners in the ring - and within that time they wanted him to kill her. It took far too long for her guard to be dropped for him, and with his last bits of strength before total paralysis he tore into her back, putting an end to her for good. It took months for his mind to return, but when it did he became just as cruel a puppet-master. Torturing sinners into obedience, ripping pieces of their minds out, tearing them apart, until death became a luxury. Only the worst of the worst are doomed to the fate that nearly befell him.

Reaper of Treachery, Rook Venus

Rook Venus was a jurigumo who hated one thing about life: its fixation on love and soulmates. They were constantly told something was wrong with them, something about them needed to be fixed, just because they couldn’t be swept off their feet by simple gestures of basic decency or grandiose displays of unwanted affection. They openly mocked the thought that their soul could be tied to another person so tightly that they were destined to be with each other. It was stupid and borderline abusive. If their soul was bound to another’s, then that person was to die.

Even in death they believe this, now blessed with the ability to rip sinners’ emotions straight out of their body and destroy them. In an ironic twist of fate, they’ve chosen to manifest this ability in various colored strings, red being their propensity for feeling love. They take great pleasure in watching the color drain from their victims’ faces as they learn what it was like to be them for all their life, so obviously they don’t do this to too many people at once. It’s far easier to break someone if they think you’re the only person they can cling to.

Wanderer of Treachery, Kudur Archae

Kudur Archae is one of those people. You know the ones. Only into anything for how xe benefits from it. Xe has always been like this. Xe views xirself as a king of the darkest magics, anything from soul manipulation, wanderer summoning, and forcing the dead to persist beyond their deaths. Xe searched out these texts, obsessing over the forbidden knowledge and doing anything to learn more. Over time, xir body has degraded from overuse of this, naught much more than a worn porcelain doll. Still, xe laughs at the face of permanent death. “I will return, I am immortal.”

Xir horrific form limps around ancient, cursed places in Weodirrus, unable to even leave if summoned. No one can tell if xe’s able to feel anything about what’s become of xirself, as xe refuses to discuss anything but business. Xe will laugh and mock those making deals with xir, although xir will negotiate exact terms down to the letter. Xe doesn’t just want your soul, xe wants any favor xe can squeeze from you, and if you can match xir offers you are guaranteed a deal.

inspiration
  • The ring was based on how abusive circuses used to be. The Greatest Showman lied for the sake of Disney.
  • These designs are the least cohesive because they don't work well together at all, thesy are the work equivalent of trying to cook popsicles in an oven on a cutting board.
  • Belial was designed to look uncanny and pathetic - because that's what he is. His basis is in the Bloody Mary rumors.
  • Rook was based on how stupid the concept of soulmates actually is and subverting the red string of fate.
  • Kudur was based on the Lich designs in Heroes of Might and Magic V and the hero "The King of Kings" in the up and coming Heroes Olden Era.
