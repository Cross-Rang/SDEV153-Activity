Fraud

The Hospital of Hell

_________

Contrary to its title, the Ring of Fraud is not a hospital, it is a research center tucked away in the city of Limbo. If you are a demon experiencing a serious medical emergency, the Ring of Fraud cannot help you, please go to an actual hospital. However, Fraud makes massive progress in understanding the treatment of demons in medical facilities - using scammers and liars as their test subjects. There are no animal protection laws to limit experiments, there are no protestors to object to their operations. Scientists and doctors collaborate to see if something is even possible. Each sentient souled species has a demonic counterpart with a similar body layout. They wouldn’t want to waste perfectly good research material, now would they?

Ideas come and go as the apparition tours the other rings of hell and procedures move from concept to established. Some concepts are clearly just for torture, others have actual backing. Egos, Ids, and Superegos bend to their fickle whims for entertainment - not out of loyalty or fear, just out of genuine concern for their mental health. The fraud ring is often brushed aside as the weakest of all the rings, however this is the ring that has caused the most destruction to the country this particular hell services.

_________

Ego
Type: Undead
Role: Lax
Creation: Native

Egos are an… odd addition to the Fraud Ring. One would assume a ring of this nature would desire demons all of whom could participate in the torture of sinners. Egos… are not that. They resemble the common puppet known as worm-on-a-string, and are commonly found snivelling in the corridors of the Fraud Ring out of the common animalistic instinct of having no attention for the past few minutes. Despite being demons with a correlating living species, that being the intelligent trickster harpies, they act more like pets than sapients.

That’s not to say they aren’t dangerous, just not in the way anything else in this ring is. They’re desperate for love and affection, so any escaped patients will be tracked by squiggly needy egos. Unlike the puppets they resemble, they weigh much, much more. Once an ego has you in its sight, it will weigh you down and prevent you from even moving. These demons are often rewarded by the staff for their captures with the same affection they were searching for, so they’re very well trained.

Superego
Type: Construct
Role: Lax
Creation: Native

If you thought egos were bad, meet the superego; the rat king of the egos. These are caused when five egos get their malleable tails tied together, but unlike the rat king this does not weaken them. This makes them more competent and less in need of attention. They end up becoming the staff’s assistants after being fitted with prosthetics to make movement easier. Bringing trays of tools, organs, chemicals, and whatever to their masters. This raises many concerning questions about the nature of egos, especially since the superegos have a completely different correlating living species; the massive aquatic kraken.

Id
Type: Etheral
Role: Working
Creation: Native

Ids are considered the worst of the Fraud Ring's demons. Why? They are the only common demon able to play with souls the way apparitions and the royalty can. And unfortunately, they do not have the same restraint the apparitions and royalty do. They are able to shape-shift to whoever they are sent to torments’ worst fears, and are even able to manipulate their victims’ bodies into a mix of what they were before and the id tormenting them. They have no reservations as long as the soul remains within the body to feel all of the pain inflicted.

On top of that, ids have a nasty habit of finding ways of avoiding death no matter what is thrown at them. This rarely extends to immortality, however a few exceptions have unfortunately been found. These demons dance around the grey area of even the demons' concepts of morality. It’s hard to tell what any of their motivations are, if they have any beyond enjoying themselves.

_________

Apparition of Fraud, Krys Jelapi
Type: Ethereal
Role: Apparition
Creation: Native

Krys Jelapi was a mistake. As an id, naturally they have some strange abilities, but their ability didn’t manifest in manipulative shapeshifting like all other ids. They were cursed with practical immortality. They can only die if burnt alive, otherwise the next person who puts their clothes on will become a carbon copy of who they once were. Due to this, some see them as not having worked for their position. Others believe that this is the truest spirit of an apparition. Krys doesn’t let any of this bother them, as they’re quite obsessed with torturing the sinners that come.

In their hospice, sinners come and go. Krys’ orderlies greet new arrivals, then Krys visits and reviews their paperwork for what they consider a suitable treatment. Contrary to their claims of absolution of sins, sadistic and pointless operations nearly immediately begin. These operations range from chemical injections just to see the effects, to cutting off and replacing limbs, to even removing vital organs if they so choose. Of course, the sinners are kept alive until their bodies physically can’t handle it anymore. You can’t call it a viable experiment unless you document the results!

Reaper of Fraud, Enid Delekarte
Type: Construct
Role: Reaper
Creation: Lost

You might’ve heard of Enid, the evil mage the brave hero of Lurewick - Mina Hart - slayed. This is a lie created by the church of Lurewick to advance their vicious anti-monster propaganda. Enid was the royal magician, dealing with a king more interested in looking good then actually helping others. So, she made a deal with Virgil to fulfill an impossible job… but as her coworkers became lost souls and died she finally felt something: Pain. She tried to end the curse, but Mina didn’t want to lose her. She was trapped halfway between living and dead, while Mina protected what remained of her body. This state was agonizing. No matter how much Krys and Virgil did their best to ease her pain she’d madly attack anything that came close to her.

After a century, Enid got her wish. Mina - well, his reincarnation - finally killed her, and she could give him the goodbye she really wanted to all those years ago. But she didn’t move on. Mages lack a soul, so she couldn’t rejoin the cycle of reincarnation, but she still had a deal to pay off that cost a soul she lacked. Instead of being tortured, after a century of pain, she agreed to help Krys in their operations. It was all she had left for her life, a logical conclusion. Maybe she’d finally get closure with the friends she lost too. If only she could return their memories of who they used to be in prior cycles.

Wanderer of Fraud, Virgil Delekarte
Type: Construct
Role: Wanderer
Creation: Native?

If you were to describe Virgil in a single phrase, it would be “too much.” Despite wanderers’ already strange habits and moralities, Virgil’s behavior is even stranger than that. It acts almost, but not quite living in the most horrific way imaginable. It demands punishment for its crimes, demands to be treated by the living as if it’s one of them. They claim this erratic and confusing behavior to be because they lost something while alive that they now want to reclaim, but their records show… nothing out of the ordinary, having been doctored by the wanderer itself to give itself a past.

Even Virgil’s methods for deals are strange. Instead of waiting for a soul to go to hell, it’ll bring hell up to them, no matter the casualties caused. Even as it bellows for its actions to be hated by the sinner, it’ll prey on their suffering and attempt to coerce them into taking their own life. Their curse will not end until the sinner has completely died, returning everything back to the way it was. If one intends to trick the populace with false claims of glory, Virgil ensures they’ll be forced to come out and admit their lies, one way or the other. Even the sinners who don’t ask for their direct intervention will begin seeing them in the corner of their eyes… ever closer every time…

Inspiration
  • This whole ring is based on medical fraud.
  • The reason only one of them is dressed like a doctor is because the other two came from completely different parts of the story.
  • Krys was originally the apparition of Sloth so their design was supposed to just be "What can I slap together in like 30 minutes?" Their design only evolved because I thought the original was too boring.
  • Enid is the protagonist of a different story who got her happy ending at the very end of the planned trilogy. Her design is supposed to scream villain because her story revolves around how she was demonized after her death for not being 100% morally good
  • Virgil was grabbed from a background character design I made during Art Fight for a jokey revenge chain where Krys died and someone else's oc was put in charge. Their personality is based on my anxiety spiralings.
