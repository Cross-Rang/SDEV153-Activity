Greed

The Crown Jewel of Hell

_________

There is nothing in Hell more gorgeous than the sapphire and gold decked out Hell’s Casino, a casino built for those who hoard wealth away from those who need it most. In this casino, you do not need money to play, there is no one stopping you from playing the most controversial of games, and you are able to rack up more money than is possible in the living world. However, in order to pay for losing, you must sacrifice pieces of yourself. These consequences ramp up based on how long you’ve played; a long-time player may lose their soul on a deal a newcomer would only lose a finger to. Cheating is punished even harsher than losing. Empty and dead bodies and missing pieces are then sewn together to create the empty husks known as dolls, who then become the next dealers at the games.

No one sees the apparition unless taken to her by the golden idols. Her game of choice is craps, one of the easiest games in her opinion. Playing starts low stakes, even fun and a bit flirty if you happen to not be a man, however slowly ramps up. Sinners rarely recognize how long they’ve been playing with her until the price paid changes from chips to their soul. Once the price is set, there are no second chances. At some point, your winning streak will fail, and your soul will become another trophy in her office.

Dolls
Type: Construct
Role: Working
Creation: Native, material is Lost

Dolls. We all know what a doll is; toys for kids built of plastic or cloth. Each doll is given a name, a personality, and a story by the child playing with it. These dolls are built in a similar method, but are not something you would want to give to your child. They are not built fully of cloth or plastic, but medical waste, animated with magic to act as another game-master. Every part a sinner loses in a bet is fuel for a later construct. These bits aren’t required to be pierced together perfectly - one of the most famous dolls after all is a plush overfilled with offal - they are just required to be able to ambulate.

Golden Idols
Type: Undead
Role: Hazard
Creation: Case-By-Case

Golden Idols are deceiving. On a first glance, these individuals appear sickly, with an unnatural gold coloring for their hair, nails, and eyes. Their bodies are weak and easily injured. They move and speak normally, polite and eager to help their doll coworkers. However, once a bet is lost, they switch immediately. They become stoic and indestructible, until the debt is fulfilled. Afterwards, they relax back into their typical state.

Lost souls who are turned into golden idols fare the same, however their emotional state is… different. They never change emotions when activated, only goals. Most sinners who’ve been in the ring for a while avoid tables with a lost soul idol at it, allegedly it’s worse to lose to someone who knows what they’re doing rather than to something that doesn’t.

Apparition of Greed, Dolcetto Arania
Type: Ethereal
Role: Apparition
Creation: Lost

A secretive and reclusive woman, Dolcetto simply refuses to share her past. It was a terrible one, so terrible she simply just wants to forget. As such she resorts to hiding away everything to keep the remainder of her heart intact. Yet, she wasn’t selected for no reason. It’s hard to wrap one’s head around why a woman so desperate to forget would be petrified of death.

Dolcetto now controls the ring of Greed, a rich place with the crown jewel being Hell’s Casino. For all the gold and glory promised to them, the price is painful: you don’t pay with money, you pay with your life. Rotting bins of organs, missing limbs, and other extremities can be seen just beyond the ring, and each doll holds trophies of their players’ losses. Dolcetto’s own price? If you lose at her craps game, she’ll have your soul as a personal trophy until a new cycle begins. There is no escape once you start playing; Dolcetto will always get her due. No matter the method, the apparition will always get her way. She will never lose ever again.

Reaper of Greed, Saturn Maggoro
Type: Ethereal
Role: Reaper
Creation: Lost

Saturn Maggoro was simply a gentleman in life, an Ovexian immigrant to Weodirrus during the short-lived gold rush where he was eventually added to the death toll. Despite doing his damndest to remain cordial and polite, quieting any conflict to the best of his ability, his natural talent for mining gold (as he immigrated from a place where the practices were far more common) bred resentment towards him. Despite his best attempts to talk the men down, he was eventually tortured, robbed, and executed by the same miners he worked so hard to befriend. His body was never found, simply rotting away in the river where he was hidden.

Despite being in Hell for half a century at this point, he’s still not used to the ins and outs at the place. For how he treats his position and… situation, you’d assume he was still that sweet Ovexian immigrant in a Weodirrian gold mine and not Dolcetto’s ruthless executioner. He’s awkward about nearly everything about his identity as it clashes so much with who he used to be. His fate is almost ironic; in life he was too much of a pushover to fight back against anyone, but now he physically can’t fight back against anything asked of him by his ring outside of a small, “Are you sure?” This role doesn’t seem to have been one he took on his own volition.

Wanderer of Greed, Zeta Jupiter
Type: Construct
Role: Wanderer
Creation: Native, material was Lost

As with the rest of the dolls, Zeta was built from the pieces of sinners who lost the games in the Hell’s Casino. Unlike them, Zeta was never exactly completed. They were left a pasty mush of the parts that were handled too roughly by the golden idols. In an attempt to look as cute as the other dolls around them, they took up sewing to create themself an adorable body that matched the completed dolls around them. Their large size made this difficult, but eventually - and with a little help from the other dolls - they achieved their dream body. They’re known around the casino as a little ray of heaven’s sunshine taken form.

Zeta is called upon often; as the Wanderer of Greed, people assume she’ll be the way they become the next billionaire. While Zeta will always honor a deal, she’ll make them wish she never honored it. Riches can’t just be obtained without hardship. The deaths of a few close relatives certainly won’t be too big of a price, and the mass slaughter of innocents shouldn’t be too hard for someone only concerned with what they want control of. If they are backtalked, begged for the contract to be nulled, or for them to take it away, they simply laugh at the joke. They weren’t concerned about how they’d gain their desires seconds ago, why start now?

Inspiration
  • This whole area is based on the red version of Through the Eyes of Desperation. I did not like the black version.
  • Each character is based on something people collect. Wine, Precious Stones, Dolls.
  • Dolcetto was supposed to be based on her namesake wine. Then I changed her color palatte because I had too much red and I'm not a fan of the combonation of Red/Gold. I'm pretty sure you can guess my thoughts on her.
  • Saturn was based on, well, Saturn. Basically I made a design for a human Saturn, got attached, gave him a hat and wrinkles, and called it a day.
  • Zeta is based on the trope of something horrific badly disguised as something cute, like Mikikyu. Specifically they are a gargatuan pile of offal trying to look like a cute human.
