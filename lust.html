Lust

Hell's Cesspit

_________

The ring of lust is a dark, decrepit red-light zone saved for those who used their power over others to abuse them. Practically every crime you can commit is welcomed with open arms here, with seedy motels sheltering sinners and the large population of incubi and succubi luring them into shady establishments for sinners to work out “pent up frustrations” so to speak. One batch of sinners becomes what the next look forward to toying with. This ring is heavily managed and guarded by the apparition, as the nature of it could cause one bad actor to become the exact thing they are trying to cull.

Nothing in this ring can be considered beautiful. The residents resemble grubs and worms far more than people, the buildings are crumbling and rotting, covered in thick meat moss that stinks of blood and rot. The only beautiful thing in the ring is a flytrap - the apparition’s chambers. The two halves give fliers to the worst, appointments to visits. One half encourages abusers to attempt to use him despite knowing how deadly and powerful he is simply by acting the part of another sinner - ditzy and unaware of the danger he has put himself in - which works far more often than one would expect. The other half forces liars - those who abuse the severity of their accusations to destroy others - to go through what they claimed until the guilt kills them. She will not physically hurt them, but many fear her more than her other half.

Ubi (Yoo-Bee)
Type: Undead
Role: Working
Creation: Native

Ubi are one of the oldest races of demons with many different forms, with two main subspecies: succubus and incubus. The biggest shared trait is their quadrupedal stance, leech-like head and the pseudo-mouths on their tails. Succubi are typically upwards of five feet tall and very reliant on others for safety and care. Incubi are massive, upwards of fifteen feet, and built with many more natural weapons than the succubi such as sharper claws and teeth. They are very intelligent and manipulative demons, tending to form relationships based on what they gain from the relationship rather than typical affection. Because of this, the amount of partners one has at any given time is seen as a source of status. Marriage is unheard of from almost every ubi, as it's seen as one cutting themself off from any further patners.

One interesting part of the ubi race is their life cycle. When a ubi is born, once they’re weaned off of their birth mother, they’re practically abandoned and left to fend for themselves. It’s speculated that the subspecies traits begin forming based on their environments; succubi forming from ubi who found any permanent community as opposed to incubi who were left to fend for themselves. Ubi are also genetically compatible with most other demons in hell, with hybrid ubi very common to see in any ring. Typically though, these hybrids take many more traits from their non-ubi parent than their ubi parent.

Apparition of Lust, Aphrodite, Epithet Urania and Epithet Pandemos
Type: Undead
Role: Apparition
Creation: Lost

Aphrodite is the name of the vessel inhabited by Epithets Urania and Pandemos. No one has heard from the true Aphrodite since… that day. No one wants to discuss what that day entailed, all anyone knows - or says - is that on that day, the at-the-time newest Apparition disappeared. The person they thought was Aphrodite was Urania trying to pick up her host’s life, only discovered when Pandemos desired his own freedom from Urania’s desperate attempts to suppress him back to their subconscious. They don’t talk much, either with each other or with their fellow apparitions. Both fear something about the other, but refuse to discuss what.

They are in charge of the ring of Lust, and take very different approaches to it. Pandemos simply seduces sinners who want to feel powerful and makes them think he’s weak, dumb, and easy to take advantage of, before ripping them to shreds once they try. Urania is far more assertive, taking the simpering messes of sinners that used lies of assault as a way to tear apart those they hated and letting them feel exactly the type of pain that they claimed to have felt. Mental and physical wounds open without a source. The more they plead for it to end, the worse it becomes until the guilt kills them… or they take matters into their own hands.

Reaper of Lust, The Collective, Epithet Nimble
Type: Construct
Role: Reaper, Hazard
Creation: Lost

The Collective consists of all of the rotted meat strewn throughout the layer of Lust, from the fleshy webbing smeared onto the sides of brothels to the sludgey piles hidden away in all the dark alleyways. One wouldn’t assume this meat to be alive at a first glance, however this is where many sinners’ first and last mistake is made. The Collective is a conglomerate of dead mimics; when their morphs fail and the webbing they’re made of melts, they join The Collective as just another voice in the cacophony. Any sinner who makes the mistake of touching the Collective is quickly drained of all their bodily fluids, left as a dry husk where they once stood.

However, the Collective isn’t the only mass comprising the reaper. One sickly body had to be amputated to protect the rest of the body, and the wanderer Cider gave it a new form using scrap metal and borrowed medical equipment. This robotic husk - known as Nimble - shares an intrinsic link to the Collective from which it was born, yet the Collective cannot sense its attachment. This tiny robot is Cider’s link back home, able to communicate what is happening through the many eyes of the body it was once part of, and its favored weapon to collect souls in Weodirrus. After all, their core still requires the nutrients their severing deprived them of.

Wanderer of Lust, Cider Junker
Type: Undead
Role: Wanderer
Creation: Native

Between the constant game of love played by the desperate succubi and manipulative incubi, one soul had always stood out: Cider Junker, an incredibly attractive incubus who seemed completely unswayed with any act of love or sex, and preferred spending its time alone building and programing robots using scrapped living technology. This isn’t to say it never engaged in these pastimes, it’s a mother of 25, but it never truly enjoyed them. The closest relations it has are the strictly platonic friends it’d made while on the job and the Epithet Nimble. Mostly, it took the role of wanderer to get out of the ring it was born into and focus on its preferred craft.

This isn’t to say it doesn’t enjoy its job, quite the opposite. It hardly has to do anything due to a contract it made while still new on the job almost 200 years ago. A family fortune built on the sacrifice of innocent souls - a fortune given to a family of half-dragons who need to kill and eat souls anyways, currently in the hands of an aristocrat practically collecting other depraved carnivores. It hardly needs to raise a finger to reach the quota, although when it does it simply relies on its favorite little robot. This gives it plenty of time to focus on its own hedonism - practicing body modifications, experimenting with the tech of the living, and ultimately not engaging in anything sexual in the slightest beyond watching pathetic souls humiliate themselves for attention it’ll never give them.

inspiration
  • The ring as a whole was made with the mental image of "The last place you want to go to." Think Gary, Indiana mixed with Vita Carnis
  • The multiple identities for 2/3 charcters was purposeful. Aphrodite was created with DID and I wanted the rest of the ring to mirror this fact.
  • Urania was created as the opposite of the traditional "Lust Demon" design. No big chest, no skimpy clothes, not ungodly horny, she's literally just a normal slime. Pandemos was designed to play this straight despite Urania's body modifications which is why he isn't shown (that and formatting issues).
  • The Collective was heavily based on Vita Carnis, specifically the crawl. Nimble was thrown in with it simply because I like meat robots and is the only one shown because I can not draw buildings to save my life.
  • Cider was based on Roadhog Overwatch. I don't play Overwatch I just think he's cool. It was also meant as a subversion to the stereotypical lust demon in the sense that normal people don't want it and it doesn't want you either.
