The Slaughterhouse of Hell

_________

Bloodsports at 470 mH. A ring procedurally operated with such horrific efficiency that even the other rings give it side-eye. The apparition rationalizes this as basic logic - the violent have no qualms about who they target. If you are not with them, obedient to them, you are against them and are just another target for them. This is safer for everyone in Hell. Each sinner is sorted into their species, and kept under until their species is due for the tournament. To prepare them, each sinner in a tournament has their appearance filed down to the “standard” of the species and each group is given a celebration before the tournament begins. No weapons, no mercy. The winner progresses until only one sinner is left to be slaughtered by the apparition. Their numbers whittle down horrifically fast - the longest any tournament has lasted with the current apparition was two weeks - and clean-up takes even less time.

The worst thing about the ring however, is that the tournaments are broadcasted all over hell. Every demon is able to watch a living being become reduced into a mutilated corpse while the charismatic reaper discusses exactly what did the victim in. These broadcasts are unavoidable even if you do your best to avoid it; every channel in hell has mentioned them at least once and the reaper has appeared on many other programs when not required to be in the ring. Every life, reduced to mere ratings.

Clocks
Type: Construct
Role: Working
Creation: Lost

Clocks are lost souls, victims of prejudice, hate crimes, and just plain violence. To protect them, they’ve been fitted in mechanical armor - essentially having become cyborgs. They aren’t… required to participate in ring activities though. Unlike other lost souls, their existence isn’t bound to torturing sinners. They’re allowed to treat hell as a new home. A second chance for them to live a normal, fulfilling life.

Apparition of Violence, Apocalypse.Aries
Type: Construct
Role: Apparition
Creation: Native

Apocalypse is an AI built with the express purpose of sheer power and violence, specifically guarding the Ring of Violence. His creator knew, as flesh and blood, they were more vulnerable to the dangers of the job even as an apparition, but an invulnerable machine would keep the ring protected from any sinner desperate to abuse their power on the clocks - all of which were former victims of extreme violence. He trained as the reaper, and took over the ring when the inevitable happened, moments after his creator passed. He barely got a chance to mourn as time passed him by and the violence of the living world only grew with more eyes on potential targets.

He holds dominion over the ring he was created to defend, treating anyone sent to him as cattle to the slaughter. Sinners are depersonalized, removed of most if not all distinct markings, and given one night of freedom before the tournament begins. These tournaments take place over the course of several weeks, segregated based on species. The rules are simple; No weapons other than your body parts and to win your opponent has to die. Apocalypse watches these from up high, analyzing its future competition. The final fight is always against him. It always ends the same way.

Reaper of Violence, Lucian Phobos
Type: Construct
Role: Reaper
Creation: Lost
p>Lucian Phobos has an extremely troubled past, once being a transgender human man. He once had the perfect life - a girlfriend into criminal justice, a promising future in advancements in mobility devices for disabled folks like himself, a family who loved and supported him. It was assumed Lucian would be immortalized in medical textbooks forever… until his lifeless body was found buried in a shallow grave, brutalized beyond recognition. Lucian's death was never solved - exactly what his killer had wanted ever since she met him.

When Lucian woke up in hell, he was still betrayed, terrified, and mortified. When given the chance to become a clock, become a reaper, and an opportunity to avenge himself, he agreed with glee. His remaining skin was flayed off, replaced by sterile bronze plating and retro digital screens, his voice made deeper and synthetic, and his non-functioning legs chopped away, granted a new wheelchair. All these new features made him resemble a demon far more than a human. The other demons in hell grew to adore him as an idol. Every day a human tournament is still in session, he eagerly browses their files for the name of the one who did this to him.

Wanderer of Violence, Hydra Deimos
Type: Construct
Role: Wanderer
Creation: Lost

The online music scene was no stranger to frustrations leveled towards those just trying to share their craft. Hydra Deimos was one of these creators, simply a young girl just trying to share the music she made with the world. Unfortunately, all eyes turned to her in the worst light, simply because she didn’t like another creator. Everything she did was unnaturally buried in hateful fans, constantly endangering her to the public. One day, she simply couldn’t do it anymore. She posted a goodbye, and was gone forever. All the other creators in her space were horrified, but ultimately nothing was done about it and Hydra was nothing but a fleeting, hated memory.

Hydra still wanted to perform though, to create a showstopping conclusion to her life that she was never able to share. And so, enthusiastically, she agreed to become a wanderer, modeling her new appearance on her favorite online idol with a few more blades and spiked maces attached. Those fans who desired a more supernatural solution to creators they hated soon were met in horror as they came face to face with an entity with a message - Hydra Deimos was back, and back with a vengeance. Creators were forced to wrangle their fan bases in horror, and now her life’s work can be summed up in a single sentence: “What did you think would happen?”
